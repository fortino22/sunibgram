# Rules & Community Guidelines

What's allowed, who can be here, and how reports work. Sources: acceptable-use from
`/about/terms`; report categories from
`backend/infrastructure/seeder/violation_seeder.go`.

---

## Eligibility (hard rule)
- **Current Binus students only.** A verified `@binus.ac.id` email is required.
- **One account per student.** No duplicate accounts.
- Verification must be completed before you can post or reply.
- Accounts that become ineligible (e.g. no longer a Binus student) may be suspended or
  terminated.

## Acceptable use — you agree NOT to:
- Harass, threaten, dox, or impersonate other students.
- Post content that is illegal, sexually explicit, violent, or designed to incite hate.
- Share academic-dishonesty material (exam leaks, paid assignment services, etc.).
- Spam, scrape, or otherwise automate the service without written permission.
- Circumvent moderation actions, blocks, bans, or rate limits.
- Attempt to break into, probe, or stress-test the service outside the
  security-report process.
- Use Sunibgram for commercial promotion that isn't clearly a campus event or
  community.

Breaking these rules can lead to content removal, feature restriction, or account
suspension/termination.

## Report categories
When reporting a thread, message, or user, you pick one of these categories (plus a
short explanation):

| Category | What it covers |
|---|---|
| **Spam** | Repetitive, misleading, or unwanted promotional content. |
| **Harassment** | Bullying, intimidation, threats, or targeted abusive behavior. |
| **Hate Speech** | Content attacking people based on protected characteristics. |
| **Misinformation** | False or misleading information that may cause harm. |
| **Nudity/Sexual Content** | Explicit sexual content, nudity, or sexually suggestive material. |
| **Violence** | Graphic violence, threats, or content encouraging physical harm. |
| **Copyright Infringement** | Content that appears to violate intellectual property rights. |
| **Other** | Other policy violations not covered by the listed categories. |

## How to report
Three-dot menu on any thread or profile → **Report** → choose a category → add a short
explanation. Reports go to the moderation queue, usually reviewed within a couple of
working days.

## Reporting a trending topic
Trends can be reported separately (source:
`backend/infrastructure/seeder/trend_report_reason_seeder.go`). Reasons:
- **The associated content is not relevant** (`not_relevant`)
- **This trend is spam** (`spam`)
- **This trend is abusive or harmful** (`abusive_or_harmful`)
- **Not interested in this** (`not_interested`)
- **This trend is a duplicate** (`duplicate`)
- **This trend is harmful or spammy** (`harmful_or_spammy`)

## Enforcement ladder
Depending on severity and history: warnings → content removal → feature restrictions →
temporary suspension → permanent account termination.

## Communities
Community owners and the admins they appoint enforce rules within their own rooms.
Sunibgram steps in only if a community is used to break these platform rules.
