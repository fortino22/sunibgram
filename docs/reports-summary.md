# How Sunibgram Runs on Fox

**Sunibgram** is a campus social platform. **Fox** is the AI agent that helps run it behind the scenes — reviewing official events and keeping leadership informed, without anyone having to dig through dashboards all day.

Fox does two main jobs:

- **Reviews every event.** When a club or organizer submits an official event, Fox reads the proposal, checks it against Sunibgram's event policy, and makes a call — **approve**, **decline**, or **ask for changes** — with a confidence score and a written reason. Low-risk, clear-cut events can be auto-approved; anything risky is flagged for a human to confirm.
- **Reports what's happening.** Fox turns all the platform's raw activity into two easy-to-read reports: a quick **morning brief** and a deeper **weekly story**. Every number comes straight from live platform data — Fox does the reading, ranking, and explaining, so leadership starts each day already knowing what matters.

The rest of this page covers those two reports.

---

## The Daily Report — "What needs attention today"

Lands in your inbox every morning and covers the last 24 hours. Think of it as your morning-coffee briefing: open it, and in two minutes you know exactly what's waiting on you.

**What's inside:**

1. **At-a-glance summary** — A short paragraph with the day's headlines: the key event decisions, the most urgent bug, and the top feature idea worth your attention.

2. **Quick numbers** — Four simple stat cards at the top:
   - Events decided
   - Events auto-approved
   - Open bugs
   - Feature requests

3. **Event decisions** — A clear table of every event waiting for review. For each one, Fox shows its call (**approve**, **decline**, or **ask for changes**), how confident it is, a quality score, and a short note explaining why.

4. **Bugs — most urgent first** — A ranked list of open bugs, with how urgent each one is and a suggested fix, so the scariest problems rise to the top automatically.

5. **Feature requests — by impact** — A ranked list of what users are asking for, scored by how much each idea could boost activity and keep users happy.

6. **Recommended actions** — A short, smart to-do list of the day's best moves.

---

## The Weekly Report — "The story of the week"

Arrives Saturday morning and covers the last 7 days. This is the big-picture view — full of charts and a community leaderboard — so you can spot trends, catch problems early, and celebrate your most active users.

**What's inside:**

1. **At-a-glance summary** — A paragraph capturing the week's trends, safety patterns, and the community members who stood out.

2. **Quick numbers** — Four stat cards at the top:
   - Reports
   - New bugs
   - Feature requests
   - Trending topics

3. **What's trending** — A bar chart of the week's top hashtags, plus an insight on *why* they're rising — and whether that's healthy buzz or something to keep an eye on.

4. **Safety & violations** — Charts breaking down reports by type and reason, with a plain-language read on what the pattern means and what to do about it.

5. **Community leaderboard** — Your top 10 most-engaging users shown as a chart and a detailed table (likes, comments, and reposts they earned), plus tips on how to encourage more of that kind of activity.

6. **Bugs & feature requests** — A chart splitting bugs from feature requests, the top 5 of each by popularity, and a quick take on what users are demanding most.

7. **Recommended actions** — The week's highest-priority moves, ranked.

8. **Honest notes** — A short, transparent note about anything the data can't fully cover that week — so you always know how much to trust each number.

## Daily vs Weekly — at a glance

| | **Daily Report** | **Weekly Report** |
|---|---|---|
| **When it arrives** | Every morning | Saturday morning |
| **What it answers** | "What needs my attention today?" | "What's the story of the week?" |
| **Best for** | Fast decisions on events, bugs, and requests right now | Spotting trends, safety patterns, and top community members |
