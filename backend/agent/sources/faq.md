# FAQ

Most-asked questions and answers. Source: `/about/help`
(`frontend/app/about/help/page.tsx`). Full help page: `/about/help` (see [[public-routes]]).

---

### Who can sign up for Sunibgram?
Only current Binus students with a verified **@binus.ac.id** email address. Accounts
are locked to one per student, and verification is required before you can post or
reply.

### I never received my OTP email. What now?
Check your spam folder first — university filtering sometimes catches our mail. If
it's still missing after a few minutes, request a new code from the verification
screen. Repeated failures usually mean your @binus.ac.id mailbox is full or your
faculty has temporarily blocked external sender domains.

### Can I change my username after sign up?
Your display name and profile details can be edited any time from profile settings
(`/settings/account`). Usernames (the `@handle`) are stable to prevent identity
confusion across mentions — reach out to support if you have a strong reason to change
yours.

### How do public and private communities differ?
Public communities let any verified Binus student join with one tap and see all
threads. Private communities require the owner or an admin to approve each join
request, and only members can read threads inside them.

### Why are some events marked "Official" and others not?
Official events have been reviewed and approved by an admin from the faculty or
student council that submitted them. Unofficial events are open to anyone to publish —
they show up in the feed, just without the verified badge.

### What counts toward my leaderboard score?
Replies you receive count the most, followed by reposts and likes from other verified
students. Posting frequently helps, but quality interactions weigh heavier than raw
post volume. Bots and self-engagement are filtered out.

### How do I report a thread, message, or user?
Use the three-dot menu on any thread or profile and pick **Report**. You'll select a
violation category and add a short explanation. Reports go to the moderation queue and
are usually reviewed within a couple of days. See [[moderation-and-reports]] for the
report categories, and [[rules]] for acceptable use.

### Can I delete my account?
Yes — open account settings (`/settings/account`) and choose **Delete account**. Your
threads, replies, communities, and messages are removed permanently from the active
app. Some logs may be retained for a short period for safety and legal reasons, as
described in the Privacy Center ([[privacy]]).
