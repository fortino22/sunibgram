# Rules & Community Guidelines

What's allowed and who can be here. Source: acceptable-use from `/about/terms`. The
**report mechanics, categories, trend-report reasons, and enforcement ladder** live in
the [[moderation-and-reports]] concept page; this page is the acceptable-use summary.

---

## Eligibility (hard rule)
- **Current Binus students only.** A verified `@binus.ac.id` email is required.
- **One account per student.** No duplicate accounts.
- Verification must be completed before you can post or reply.
- Accounts that become ineligible (e.g. no longer a Binus student) may be suspended or
  terminated.

See [[eligibility-and-verification]] for the full sign-up / verification flow.

## Acceptable use — you agree NOT to:
- Harass, threaten, dox, or impersonate other students.
- Post content that is illegal, sexually explicit, violent, or designed to incite hate.
- Share academic-dishonesty material (exam leaks, paid assignment services, etc.).
- Spam, scrape, or otherwise automate the service without written permission.
- Circumvent moderation actions, blocks, bans, or rate limits.
- Attempt to break into, probe, or stress-test the service outside the
  security-report process.
- Use Sunibgram for commercial promotion that isn't clearly a campus event or
  community.

Breaking these rules can lead to content removal, feature restriction, or account
suspension/termination.

How to report, the full report-category list, trend-report reasons, and the

[[communities|Community]] owners and the admins they appoint enforce rules within their
