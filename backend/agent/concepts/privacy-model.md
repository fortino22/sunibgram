# Privacy model

Who can see your content. The full plain-language notice is in [[privacy]]; the binding
text is at `/about/privacy`.

## Visibility follows where you posted
- **Public threads** — visible to any verified Sunibgram user.
- **Community threads** — visible only to verified members of that [[communities|community]].
- **Direct messages & group rooms** — visible only to the people you sent them to (see
  [[messages]]).
- **Faculty feeds** — posts by people in your [[faculties|faculty]]; only verified
  students see them.

## Private account
Switching to a **private account** (`/settings/privacy`) means only people you approve
see your full profile and posts.

## Your controls
Edit profile/avatar/banner; block or unfollow any user; unsend messages (removes them for
everyone); switch to private; delete your account (removes your threads, replies, and
messages from the active service). Settings routes are in [[user-routes]]; data-export
requests go to `support@sunibgram.com`.
