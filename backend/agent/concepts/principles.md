# The six principles

What Sunibgram stands for (source: `/about/our-story` + `/about/company`).

1. **Verified, always** — every account is tied to a real `@binus.ac.id` email. No bots,
   no anonymous trolls, no off-campus strangers. See [[eligibility-and-verification]].
2. **Built around campus** — [[faculties|faculty]], batch, and community are first-class.
   The feed knows the difference between a classmate and a stranger.
3. **Student-first** — made by Binus students, for Binus students.
4. **No engagement-bait** — no recommended outrage, no dark patterns, no infinite vanity
   metrics. The feed is for catching up, not doom-scrolling. See [[feed-ranking]].
5. **Easy to find your people** — communities, faculty feeds, and search make it simple
   to land in the right room, even in your first week.
6. **Honest about what we are** — a student-built project, not an official Binus product.
   [[independence]] keeps us honest.
