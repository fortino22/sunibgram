# Leaderboard

Top contributors at `/leaderboard` (see [[user-routes]]).

## Reset
Rankings refresh **weekly**, resetting **Sunday at 00:00** (local time).

## Scoring
Replies you receive weigh heaviest, then reposts and likes from other verified students.
Quality interactions outweigh raw post volume. Bots and self-engagement are filtered out.

## Faculty boards
A separate ranking per faculty (see [[faculties]]), plus your own week-over-week stats.
